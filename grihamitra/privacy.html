GrihaMitra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

GrihaMitra is a mobile app from Kulal Labs, the brand name of an independent app developer based in India. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Identifiers and account details. When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous user ID, so you can use it without signing up. If you choose to create an account or sign in, we also process your email address and display name. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication.

Information you enter. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Budget details, such as monthly income, monthly expenses, savings, loan tenure and interest rate
  • Property preferences, such as property type, size, budget, preferred localities and possession timeline
  • Your progress on checklists
  • Payment and expense entries, including labels, amounts, dates and notes

This information is saved on your device and, even if you haven't created an account, synced to our servers using your user ID. This helps keep it backed up and available when you sign in on another device.

AI assistant messages. When you use the AI assistant, your messages and related topic information are sent to a third-party AI service to generate a reply. Chat history is not saved between app launches.

Purchase information. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive and store details needed to provide your plan, such as the plan type, status, expiry date and purchase verification information. We do not receive your full card or bank details.

App settings on your device. The app saves settings such as your language, city, theme and acceptance of our disclaimer on your device.

Technical information. Our service providers may process technical information, such as IP address and app or device identifiers, to run and secure the services used by the app.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It does not currently include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features and save your progress
  • To sync your information between your device and our servers, and across your devices
  • To generate AI assistant replies
  • To verify purchases and unlock paid features
  • To respond to support requests and keep the app secure
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run the app, or where the law requires it. Our service providers are:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, and backend services
  • Google Gemini, through Firebase AI Logic: AI assistant replies
  • Google Play: app distribution, purchases and subscription verification

These services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Using the AI assistant safely: depending on the service terms that apply, Google may keep AI messages and use them to improve its services, and people may review them. Please do not include sensitive or identifying details, such as your Aadhaar or PAN number, bank details, or property documents.

Where your information is stored

Your synced app data is stored using Google Cloud services in India (the asia-south1 region). Our service providers may process some information in other countries.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access rules that limit synced data to your own account. No app or online service can guarantee complete security.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information while you use the app or until you ask us to delete it. We may keep limited records, such as purchase information, where needed for legal, tax, security or dispute reasons. Uninstalling the app removes data saved on your device, but does not delete data on our servers.

Your rights and choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or raise a concern about how it has been handled. You may have further rights under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

To request account and data deletion, email akshatha1001@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my GrihaMitra data". Include your account email address. If you use the app without an account, include your user ID if the app shows one, or tell us how else we can identify your data. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

Children's privacy

GrihaMitra is intended for adults planning to buy a home and is not directed at anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Links to other services

The app may link to other websites and services, such as government portals. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us

For privacy questions, requests or grievances, email us.
