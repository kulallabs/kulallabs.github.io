Free to start

GrihaMitra is free to download. The budget planner, property preferences, property evaluation checklist and a limited AI assistant trial are free. A paid plan unlocks the full journey, and you'll see the price before you buy.

Good to know

GrihaMitra is an informational and organisational tool, not legal, financial or professional advice.

Checklists, fees, rates and processes are general guidance and may change or be incomplete for your situation. Please confirm the details with a qualified professional, your lender or the relevant government office before acting.
