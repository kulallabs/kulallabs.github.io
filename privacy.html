Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Kulal Labs is the brand name of an independent app developer based in India. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit this website (kulallabs.github.io) or contact Kulal Labs.

Our apps

Each Kulal Labs app has its own privacy policy explaining what data that app uses and why. The policy is linked from the app's page on this website and from its Google Play listing. If an app's policy differs from this one, the app's policy applies to your use of that app.

Information we collect

This website has no accounts or forms, and you can browse it without giving us any personal information. It does not use its own cookies, analytics or advertising.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use your information

We use the information in your email only to reply to you, provide support, and keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. Please see the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Sharing your information

We share your information only with the service providers needed to run this website and our email, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep your information

We keep emails only for as long as we need them to help you, keep appropriate records, or meet legal requirements.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and raise a concern about how it has been handled. To do this, email us using the address below. You may have further rights under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email Mail Us.
